FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What's included in the price?

Each package includes design, development, responsive optimization, basic SEO, hosting setup assistance and post-launch support. Specific features vary by package—see details above.

How long does a project take?

Starter projects typically take 2-3 weeks, Professional 4-6 weeks, and Enterprise timelines are determined based on scope. We'll provide a detailed timeline during consultation.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We typically split projects into milestones: 50% upfront to begin work and 50% upon completion. Custom payment arrangements can be discussed for larger projects.

Can I update the website myself?

Absolutely. Professional and Enterprise packages include CMS integration and training so you can manage content independently. We also offer ongoing support if needed.

Do you provide hosting?

We help you choose and set up appropriate hosting for your needs, but hosting fees are separate. We can recommend quality providers and handle the technical setup.

What if I need more features later?

We build scalable websites that can grow with your business. Additional features can be added after launch. Contact us to discuss your evolving needs.
